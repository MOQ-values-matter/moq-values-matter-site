# moq-values-matter-site
MOQ Values Matter — a living value knowledge atlas grounded in the Metaphysics of Quality

Values have been central to my thinking since reading Robert Pirsigs work. His insights fundamentally shifted my perception and thinking to such a degree, that it can only be called a Quality event. After 7 years at university battling post-modernism, relativity and power - a literary work acted as the missing piece tying all those loose ends together. Putting it all in its place.

This site is my attempt to further build a foundation based on the principles established by Robert Pirsig. Values are notoriously difficult to discourse as we are "immersed" in them - and because of this - few pursue a metaphysical foundation at all. We can feel the effect of quality. We live quality. We know quality. Still not many have pondered - why? And is there something that is better than something else?

The goal of this work is to treat values as the most fundamental part of reality, and pursue where that principle leads us. If I will achieve any quality - only time will show.
