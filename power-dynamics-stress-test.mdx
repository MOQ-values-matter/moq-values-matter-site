## Purpose

The dynamics framework v1.1 has been constructed but not yet tested against any pattern. This is the first stress-test: the framework applied to power, which was identified throughout the framework construction as the natural first test case because power surfaced the binding-relation gap most clearly during the original migration. The stress-test does three things: applies the framework's primitives to power; resolves the questions about power that were deferred to the dynamics half (self-justification, subjected-position, binding-relations); and surfaces what the framework cannot yet handle, so refinement can be planned before further patterns are tested.

The structure follows the seven-item scope agreed before the test: binding-relations formalised, corrected amplification framing applied, accumulation dynamics articulated, cascade specified, self-justification resolved, subjected-position addressed, framework gaps surfaced. The output of the stress-test feeds two file updates (VA-POW-001-power.mdx and VA-POW-001-relations.md to v0.3) and a framework-refinement section that may inform a v1.2 of the dynamics framework.

The disciplines being held throughout: the DQ-boundary discipline (analysis of power's flow is static-pattern work about a dynamic phenomenon, not a capture of becoming-itself); the stratum discipline (cascade across strata is mediated and transformative, never direct origination); the agent-field co-constitution discipline (power is not "in" the agent or "in" the field but operates as a pattern of agent-field interaction); and the non-inflation discipline on quality differences (power's amplification of bound patterns is enabling-capacity for field-recruitment, not creation from nothing).

## 1. Re-reading Power Under the Dynamics Framework

Before applying specific primitives, the broad shape of what the framework reveals about power, beyond what the statics framework could articulate.

Power, viewed through the dynamics framework, is the social-stratum pattern that most exemplifies what binding does. Power's defining structural feature — its conditionality, the way its character is set by what it is bound to — is not a peculiarity of power; it is power being a pattern whose primary mode of operation is through bindings. A pattern that operates primarily through bindings has a character determined by its bindings rather than by its own structure. This is exactly what the v0.2 entry recorded as "decisive without being directional" — power is decisive (it amplifies what it is bound to substantially) without being directional (the direction comes from the binding, not from power).

This framing also clarifies the relationship between power's statics-side conditionality and its dynamics-side operation. The conditional compositional character (statics) and the binding-driven amplification (dynamics) are two faces of the same underlying pattern: power's character relative to the field is set by what it is bound to because amplification's direction is set by the binding's target. The two halves of the framework here converge on the same structural fact about power, articulated through their different primitives.

Power's amplification is field-recruitment through binding. Power does not produce value from nothing. What power does — its defining operation — is enable the patterns it is bound to to recruit flow from the field that those patterns alone could not coordinate. Reciprocity at small scale can sustain itself through interpersonal accountability; reciprocity at the scale of millions of strangers in complex transactions cannot sustain itself through interpersonal accountability alone. Power bound to reciprocity (as institutional enforcement, rule of law, legitimate authority) enables reciprocity to recruit coordinated flow at scales reciprocity-alone could not manage. Same for legitimacy, for authority's effective exercise, for trust at scale. Power is the coordinative structure through which patterns that depend on multi-agent coordination can operate at scales that exceed the unaided coordination capacity of those patterns.

This framing is consistent with both the existing entry and with Pirsig's broader account. It does not require any claim that power "creates" anything; it requires only that power's binding enables additional flow-recruitment from the field. The field is the source of the additional flow; power's binding makes its recruitment possible.

Power's accumulation is genuine stock-formation. What makes power power rather than momentary capacity is accumulation — flow compounding into durable capacity over time. Power's accumulation operates at multiple timescales simultaneously: immediate flow (specific exercises drawing on accumulated reserves), episodic flow (campaigns building or depleting capacity), sustained flow (institutional consolidation over years), generational flow (dynastic power, institutional persistence across human lifetimes). The framework's accumulation primitive handles this directly; the temporal dimensions vocabulary handles the multi-scale character.

Power's cascade is the cross-stratum dynamics that gives power its world-shaping character. Power at the social stratum modulates the biological stratum directly (security/stress, survival pressure, biological flourishing); it cascades through biology to the physical stratum (territory, resource organisation, infrastructure); it modulates the intellectual stratum directly (enabling/suppressing intellectual patterns, institutionalising or marginalising knowledge). Each cross-stratum operation is mediated and transformative — power's social-stratum operation does not appear at lower strata as "social pressure in lower-stratum form" but transforms into the lower stratum's own character (biological stress, physical organisation, intellectual marginalisation).

Power's self-justification when unbound is a specific binding configuration, not a degradation of power's own structure. The framework's binding primitive provides the resolution: power whose bindings to reciprocity, legitimacy, and field-supportive patterns have collapsed becomes power bound only to its own perpetuation — a binding configuration that produces the internally-consonant-externally-dissonant signature in the field, but does not constitute power's own structural degradation. This is articulated in detail below.

Across all these dimensions, the framework handles power substantially. It also surfaces some refinement opportunities, addressed in §9.

## 2. Binding-Relations Articulated Formally

The v0.2 relations file recorded two binding-relations provisionally: REL-BND-POW-REC-001 (power-reciprocity) and REL-BND-POW-LEG-001 (power-legitimacy). With binding committed as a formal relation type in the dynamics framework, these are promoted to formal records, and additional binding-relations the statics analysis could not type cleanly are now articulable.

### 2.1 Power–Reciprocity (REL-BND-POW-REC-001)

The clearest case of high-quality binding in the power cluster. Power bound to reciprocity is power deployed to protect, enforce, and sustain reciprocal arrangements — what the original power entry called "enforcement" in its proper sense. The binding has the following structural features under the framework:

- **Source:** VA-POW-001 (Power)
- **Target:** VA-REC-001 (Reciprocity)
- **Directionality:** Bidirectional. Power feeds reciprocity's flow at scale (amplifying reciprocity beyond what unaided reciprocity could sustain), and reciprocity feeds power's flow (legitimacy and voluntary compliance reduce power's enforcement costs and replenish its reserves).
- **Quality:** High. Both patterns' coherent operation is supported by the binding. Reciprocity operates at scale through power's coordinative structure; power's reserves are sustained through reciprocity's voluntary compliance.
- **Strength:** Tight when active. The binding, when present, substantially shapes both patterns' operation.
- **Durability:** Standing-but-conditional. The binding persists while reciprocity's voluntary compliance continues to replenish power's reserves and while power's enforcement continues to protect reciprocity from defection. Either side's collapse degrades the binding.
- **Conditions:** The binding holds when power's exercise is constrained by reciprocity's structural requirements (enforcement of rules that apply to power-holders themselves as well as to others) and when reciprocity's recognised legitimacy supports power's continued exercise.

### 2.2 Power–Legitimacy (REL-BND-POW-LEG-001)

Power bound to legitimacy is power whose exercise is structured by the field's belief in its rightfulness. The binding amplifies in both directions:

- **Source:** VA-POW-001 (Power)
- **Target:** VA-LEG-001 (Legitimacy)
- **Directionality:** Bidirectional. Power bound to legitimacy is amplified by it (reduced enforcement cost, voluntary alignment); legitimacy bound to power is amplified by it (effective legitimate authority can act in ways recognised-but-impotent legitimacy cannot).
- **Quality:** High when both patterns are field-supportive. The binding supports both patterns' coherent operation in fields where legitimacy is grounded in actual field-supportive function.
- **Strength:** Variable. Some bindings are tight (the legitimately-recognised state); others are loose (legitimacy formally recognised but practically constrained).
- **Durability:** Standing-but-conditional. The binding persists while power's exercise remains aligned with what legitimacy claims to authorise. Misalignment erodes legitimacy and degrades the binding.
- **Conditions:** Legitimacy's continued belief in power's rightfulness, which depends on power's actual exercise remaining recognisable as serving the values legitimacy claims to authorise.

### 2.3 Power–Violence (REL-BND-POW-VIO-001) — NEW

The statics analysis recorded violence as a weak substrate-dependence (REL-SUB-POW-VIO-001) — violence is one possible physical means power can draw on. The dynamics analysis adds a binding-relation for the case when power IS bound to violence as its mode of operation, distinct from the substrate-dependence:

- **Source:** VA-POW-001 (Power)
- **Target:** VA-VIO-001 (Violence)
- **Directionality:** Directional. Power channels through violence; violence is the means, power is the standing capacity.
- **Quality:** Low. Power bound primarily to violence as its mode of operation generates counter-pattern formation (resistance, fear-based compliance that erodes under any weakening of enforcement, depletion of biological reserves of the subject population). The binding amplifies violence's reach but degrades power's reserves.
- **Strength:** Variable. Some power is bound only weakly to violence (legitimate state with violence as backstop); some power is bound tightly to violence (overt dictatorship, military rule).
- **Durability:** Contingent. Power tightly bound to violence is unstable in the durability sense; the binding consumes power's reserves faster than violence-based exercise can replenish them. Sustained tight-binding-to-violence is rare without collapse.
- **Conditions:** Power binds to violence as its mode of operation when other bindings (reciprocity, legitimacy) have failed or are deliberately rejected. Power bound to violence-as-substrate (weak dependence, violence as backstop) is structurally different from power bound to violence-as-mode (tight, primary operation).

This is the dynamics-side companion to the substrate-dependence record. The two relations are not duplicates: substrate-dependence captures the structural fact that violence is one means available to power; the binding-relation captures the specific configuration where power's primary mode of operation is through violence. Power can have the substrate-dependence without the binding (legitimate state holding violence as backstop), and could in principle have a partial binding without primary substrate-reliance (though this is rare).

### 2.4 Power–Authority (REL-BND-POW-AUT-001) — NEW

The statics analysis recorded power and authority as distinction-relations (REL-OPP-POW-AUT-001) — they are dissociable patterns; each can exist without the other. The dynamics analysis adds the binding-relation that operates when they are present together:

- **Source:** VA-POW-001 (Power)
- **Target:** VA-AUT-001 (Authority)
- **Directionality:** Bidirectional. Power bound to authority is "effective authority" — recognised right plus effective capacity to enforce. Authority bound to power is "legitimately exercised power" — capacity grounded in recognised right.
- **Quality:** High when both patterns are field-supportive. The binding supports each pattern operating where the other cannot operate alone — authority without power is hollow recognition; power without authority is unrestrained capacity.
- **Strength:** Tight when present. The configuration of legitimately-recognised effective capacity is typically tightly coupled.
- **Durability:** Standing. The binding is one of the more stable configurations in the power cluster; effective authority is durable across institutional contexts.
- **Conditions:** Holds when authority's recognition aligns with power's capacity, and when power's exercise remains within what authority authorises.

The distinction-relation and the binding-relation are not duplicates. Distinction captures that power and authority are dissociable (one can exist without the other); the binding captures what happens when they are present together (they couple, amplifying both).

### 2.5 Power–Influence (REL-BND-POW-INF-001) — NEW (promoted from flag)

The v0.2 relations file flagged that "when the dynamics half exists, influence may be more cleanly captured as a binding-relation — one of the value-flow couplings through which power feeds bound patterns — rather than as either constituent or distinction." The flag is now resolved:

- **Source:** VA-POW-001 (Power)
- **Target:** VA-INF-001 (Influence)
- **Directionality:** Directional. Power uses influence as one mode of operation; influence drawn on by power is amplified beyond what unaided influence could achieve.
- **Quality:** Variable. The binding can be high-quality (influence used to coordinate voluntary alignment around field-supportive patterns) or low-quality (influence used to manipulate the field's recognition of what power is doing).
- **Strength:** Variable. Power can be bound tightly to influence (soft-power-dominant configurations) or weakly (influence as one tool among several).
- **Durability:** Standing-but-shifting. Power's binding to influence shifts as effectiveness changes; high-quality influence-binding has greater durability than low-quality manipulative-influence-binding.
- **Conditions:** Power draws on influence when direct exercise (commanding via stakes/access/consequences) would be too costly or counterproductive, or when voluntary alignment is more durable than commanded compliance.

The distinction-relation REL-OPP-POW-INF-001 is preserved alongside the binding-relation: power and influence are dissociable patterns AND when both are present they typically couple through this binding.

### 2.6 Power–Coercion (REL-BND-POW-COE-001) — NEW

Coercion's relations file (REL-SUB-COE-PWR-001) records the cost-source side of the power-coercion relationship — coercion draws on power as one possible cost-source. The dynamics analysis adds the power-side binding for the case when power is exercised through coercion (the binding from power's side):

- **Source:** VA-POW-001 (Power)
- **Target:** VA-COE-001 (Coercion)
- **Directionality:** Directional. Power's accumulated capacity is the substrate coercion draws on; coercion's operation is one channel through which power exercises.
- **Quality:** Low. Power bound to coercion as its mode of operation is structurally low-quality. The binding amplifies coercion's reach but degrades power's reserves (counter-pattern formation reduces voluntary compliance, depleting the legitimacy and trust reserves power requires for sustained operation).
- **Strength:** Variable. Power can be loosely bound to coercion (coercion as one tool used sparingly) or tightly bound (coercion as primary mode of operation, "coercive power").
- **Durability:** Contingent and degrading. Power tightly bound to coercion erodes its own substrate over time; the binding is durable only as long as power's reserves can supply the contingent costs coercion requires.
- **Conditions:** Power binds to coercion as its mode of operation when reciprocity-based binding fails or is rejected. The binding is structurally low-quality because coercion's internally-consonant-externally-dissonant character means the binding distorts the field power operates in, which degrades the substrate power requires.

This binding is the power-side counterpart to coercion's cost-source binding. They are the same coupling viewed from two patterns; under the folder convention each lives in its own source pattern's folder. Note that under the dynamics framework's binding primitive, the relation in coercion's folder (REL-SUB-COE-PWR-001) should also be reclassified from "substrate_dependence with character conditional" to "binding with binding_kind: cost-source" — but this is a schema-update task downstream of the framework's adoption, not a power-side task.

### 2.7 Power–Self-Perpetuation (REL-BND-POW-SLF-001) — NEW

The configuration of power's self-justification when unbound. Articulating this as a binding-relation rather than as a degradation condition is the resolution of the deferred question (§7 below). Here the formal relation record:

- **Source:** VA-POW-001 (Power)
- **Target:** VA-POW-001 (Power) — itself, in the configuration of self-referential binding
- **Directionality:** Self-referential. Power bound to its own perpetuation rather than to any field-supportive pattern.
- **Quality:** Low. The binding amplifies power but supplies no field-supportive amplification — power amplifying itself without amplifying any value-pattern the field is composed of.
- **Strength:** Variable. Some power can self-perpetuate weakly while still maintaining other bindings; some power is bound primarily to its own perpetuation with other bindings degraded.
- **Durability:** Generates the internally-consonant-externally-dissonant signature: the binding persists internally (power continues to operate as power, sustaining itself) but produces field-degradation through counter-pattern formation, legitimacy erosion, and substrate exhaustion.
- **Conditions:** Emerges when other bindings (to reciprocity, legitimacy, authority) collapse or are deliberately severed, leaving power bound only to its own continued exercise.

This binding is the structural articulation of what the entry's disposition called "tends toward self-justification when unbound." It is a binding configuration rather than a degradation condition (see §7).

### 2.8 Summary of binding-relation portfolio

Power's binding portfolio (the set of bindings it has at any moment) determines its operational character. A power-holder might be bound simultaneously to reciprocity, legitimacy, authority, and (weakly) violence as substrate — this is the high-quality binding portfolio of a well-functioning legitimate state. A different power-holder might be bound primarily to coercion and self-perpetuation, with reciprocity and legitimacy degraded — this is the low-quality portfolio of an authoritarian regime in decline.

The framework's binding primitive handles each binding individually; the binding-portfolio concept (how multiple simultaneous bindings interact and what aggregate character they produce) is a refinement gap surfaced by this stress-test (see §9).

## 3. Amplification Under the Corrected Framing

The framework v1.1 corrected the amplification framing from "produces more flow than was supplied" to "the binding enables the bound pattern to recruit additional flow from the field that the bound pattern alone could not coordinate." This stress-test confirms the corrected framing is analytically clean for power.

Power amplifies reciprocity by enabling reciprocity to recruit coordinated flow at scales reciprocity-alone cannot sustain. Reciprocity in small interpersonal groups recruits flow through direct accountability — each party tracks the other's contributions and responds to imbalance. At scale (millions of strangers, complex transactions, institutional structures), direct accountability is impossible; reciprocity needs structural support to recruit flow at all. Power bound to reciprocity provides that structural support — enforcement that protects reciprocity from defection, coordinative authority that resolves disputes, institutional persistence that makes reciprocity's commitments durable across time and transactions. The flow being recruited is not produced by power; it is produced by the field's many participants engaging in reciprocal arrangements that power's structure makes durable and large-scale. Power enables; the field supplies; reciprocity organises.

The corrected framing also resolves what was previously analytically muddy: the question of how power "amplifies" something without "creating" it. Under the corrected framing, the question dissolves — power doesn't create anything, and amplification is not creation. Power binds to a pattern; the binding makes the pattern's flow-recruitment from the field possible at scales the pattern alone could not manage; the recruited flow is what is amplified. This is consistent with the agent-field co-constitution commitment (§1.4 of the framework): the field is not separate from the agents composing it; the agents' contributions are what the field can recruit; power's binding enables coordinated recruitment of those contributions through patterns that depend on coordination.

The same logic applies to power's amplification of low-quality patterns. Power bound to predation enables predation to recruit destructive flow from the field at scales unaided predation could not coordinate — large-scale resource extraction, organised exploitation, institutional capture. Same primitive (amplification through binding enabling field-recruitment), same structural operation, different valence depending on the bound pattern's character. The framework handles this without requiring any "creation" claim and without requiring any "destruction" claim — the bound pattern recruits flow effectively, and the consequences depend on whether the pattern serves or undermines the field.

The 1000-marching-on-50 example from your loyalty framing fits the same structural pattern. The 1000 are a field bound to loyalty; loyalty's binding to the field enables recruitment of coordinated agent-flow that an unloyaly-bound field of 50 cannot match, even when the 50 are individually better-equipped. Loyalty enables the field's recruitment; power-when-present amplifies this by providing additional coordinative structure. The framework's amplification primitive captures this directly.

## 4. Accumulation Dynamics

Power accumulates. This is what makes power power rather than momentary capacity. The dynamics framework's accumulation primitive handles power's accumulation, with several aspects worth articulating.

### 4.1 What power accumulates

Power accumulates several distinct stocks:

- **Coordinative capacity** — the standing capacity to organise multi-agent action through stakes, access, and consequences. This builds through successful exercise of coordination, which generates reputation, alignment, and institutionalisation that increases future coordinative capacity.
- **Reserves of compliance** — the field's voluntary alignment with power's exercise, built through reciprocity-binding and legitimacy-binding. These reserves are the substrate power draws on when exercising; sustained exercise without replenishment depletes them.
- **Reserves of resources** — material capacity (territory, infrastructure, wealth) accumulated through power's operation, which becomes substrate for further operation. The economic-flow tradition's account of capital accumulation is structurally parallel (per the survey, with appropriate non-reduction discipline — power's reserves include but are not reducible to economic capital).
- **Reputation/standing** — the field's recognition of power's capacity and effectiveness, which feeds back into voluntary alignment and reduces enforcement cost.
- **Institutional structure** — power encoded in standing structures (organisations, offices, procedures) that persist beyond individual exercises and beyond the lives of individual power-holders.

Each of these is a stock that accumulates from flow over time. The framework's accumulation primitive handles them.

### 4.2 Reinforcing feedback in power's accumulation

Power's accumulation exhibits the reinforcing-feedback configuration (§4.2 of the framework). Successful exercise of power produces results (the field is coordinated, outcomes are achieved) that increase the field's recognition of power's effectiveness; recognition reduces resistance and increases voluntary alignment; reduced resistance reduces the cost of further exercise; the next exercise is more effective. The loop compounds positively while the conditions for it remain in place (sufficient reserves, alignment with reciprocity/legitimacy, no exhaustion of substrate).

This reinforcing feedback is what makes power durable when it is well-bound and dangerous when it is poorly-bound. Power bound to predation also has a reinforcing loop (successful predation produces resources and fear, which reduces resistance and enables further predation), but the loop runs through field-degradation rather than field-support. The reinforcing loop's structural form is the same; the field-effects are very different.

### 4.3 Negative accumulation in power

Power also accumulates negatively. When power is exercised without replenishing the reserves it draws on — when accumulated compliance is consumed without being restored through reciprocity, when legitimacy is drawn down without being earned, when resources are extracted faster than they can be replenished — debt accumulates. The framework's accumulation primitive handles this directly: negative accumulation is stock-formation with opposite valence, structurally the same primitive operation.

What accumulates negatively for power: depleted compliance reserves (the field's voluntary alignment is exhausted), depleted legitimacy (the field's belief in power's rightful exercise is eroded), depleted trust reserves (the field's expectation that power will be exercised reciprocally is degraded), depleted material reserves (the substrate power depends on is exhausted), accumulated resentment (counter-pattern formation among those subject to power), accumulated incompetence (institutionalised power that no longer corresponds to actual capacity).

When negative accumulation exceeds positive accumulation, power's effective capacity degrades — even if its formal structure remains intact. This is the "collapse" configuration applied to power: reserves are depleted faster than they are replenished, eventually exceeding the field's capacity to absorb stress, at which point coherence fails. Empires fall this way. Institutions calcify this way. Persistent regimes lose effectiveness this way.

### 4.4 Multi-timescale accumulation

Power's accumulation operates at multiple timescales simultaneously (per the framework's temporal-dimensions section). A single exercise of power has immediate flow (drawing on reserves, producing immediate effect). A campaign or programme has episodic flow (building or depleting reserves across weeks to months). Institutional consolidation has sustained flow (years to decades). Dynastic power and institutional persistence have generational flow (across human lifetimes).

The same power-holder is operating on all these timescales at once. The immediate exercise draws on reserves built over decades; the decades of consolidation are sustained through generational institutional structures; the institutional structures persist across power-holders' lifetimes. The framework's multi-timescale treatment handles this; the analysis benefits from naming which timescale a particular phenomenon operates on.

### 4.5 The reserves vocabulary from Circle/Ripple Theory

Circle/Ripple Theory's **reserves** concept names exactly what the accumulation primitive produces: stored value that absorbs stress and delays collapse. Power's reserves are accumulated stock that the field's stress (sustained conflict, resource depletion, legitimacy challenges) is absorbed against. When reserves are depleted, the field's stress shows up as power's degradation rather than being absorbed.

The framework's accumulation primitive and Circle/Ripple Theory's reserves vocabulary converge cleanly: reserves are accumulated stock; depletion of reserves is the reverse direction of accumulation (flow leaving the stock faster than entering); collapse occurs when stock depletion exceeds the field's structural integrity. The vocabularies are compatible and mutually reinforcing.

## 5. Cascade Analysis

Power's cross-stratum effects are extensive, and the framework's cascade primitive handles them cleanly. The analysis distinguishes direct modulation (one stratum below power's primary stratum) from cascaded modulation (further strata reached through intermediate cascade).

### 5.1 Power's modulation of biology — direct

Power modulates the biological stratum directly (not cascaded). This is the immediate effect of power on the biological flourishing of those in its field. Power bound to protective patterns (reciprocity, legitimate authority, defence) modulates biology supportively — reduced stress, reduced survival pressure, increased security, increased resilience. Power bound to predatory patterns modulates biology distortingly — increased stress, increased fear, increased survival pressure, depleted biological reserves.

The modulation is direct because biology is the stratum immediately below social; the social-stratum operation of power affects biological substrate without requiring intermediate cascade. This is recorded in REL-MOD-POW-BIO-001 with character conditional and modulation_kind conditional. The dynamics framework adds: the modulation is direct (not cascaded), the conditionality is set by the binding portfolio (which patterns power is bound to), and the modulation operates with characteristic delays at the biological stratum (acute stress responses are fast, but chronic biological effects of sustained power-binding manifest over weeks to years).

### 5.2 Power's effect on physical — cascaded through biology and institutional structure

Power does not directly modulate the physical stratum (per the stratum discipline; direct social-to-physical modulation would be stratum-skipping cascade and is prohibited). Power's effects on physical reality run through:

- **Biological cascade**: power-modulated biology (people's actions on the physical world) produces physical effects through aggregate biological agency.
- **Institutional cascade**: power encoded in institutions directs the physical operations of those institutions (military deployment, infrastructure construction, resource extraction).

Both cascades are mediated and transformative. What appears at the physical stratum is not "social power in physical form" but transformed physical reality (territories occupied, infrastructures built, resources extracted, environments altered). The cascade discipline is held: no direct origination from social to physical, all effects mediated through intermediate strata.

### 5.3 Power's modulation of intellectual — direct

Power modulates the intellectual stratum directly (one stratum above social, going upward in modulation). Power can enable intellectual patterns to propagate, institutionalise, and scale; it can also suppress them. The modulation is direct because intellectual patterns operate above social and power's social-stratum operation reaches them directly through enabling or denying their social-substrate (institutional support, audiences, resources for development, freedom from suppression).

The conditional character of the modulation (supportive when aligned, distorting when misaligned) is set by the binding portfolio. Power bound to intellectual-supportive patterns (research, education, open inquiry) modulates supportively; power bound to control-oriented patterns (information control, suppression of dissent) modulates distortingly.

The modulation is constrained by the prohibition on cross-stratum origination: power does not generate intellectual value (truth, coherence, insight) — origination is forbidden — but determines whether intellectual patterns can spread, persist, and operate effectively at scale.

### 5.4 The cascade-delay character

All of power's cascades operate with characteristic delays. Immediate exercise of power produces visible immediate effects (a specific decision, a specific action), but the cascaded effects manifest over longer timescales. Biological effects of sustained power-modulation appear over weeks to months for acute conditions, years to decades for chronic conditions. Physical effects of cascaded action appear over months to years for built infrastructure, decades to centuries for territorial occupation, generations for environmental alteration. Intellectual effects of suppression or enabling appear over years to generations for institutional patterns.

The framework's delay primitive (from systems dynamics) handles this directly. The analysis benefits from explicit attention to which timescale a particular cascade operates on, because the immediate visible exercise can be very different from the eventual cascaded effects.

### 5.5 Buddhist dependent-origination structure applied to power's cascade

The Buddhist tradition's contribution to the framework (per §6.4) — the Twelve Nidānas as analytical structure rather than specific content — is useful here. Power's cascade across strata is best understood as a conditioning sequence rather than as direct causation. Power's exercise conditions biological substrate (people's stress, survival pressure, security); biological substrate conditioned by power conditions agents' physical action (people act differently under stress, fear, security); agents' physical action conditions physical reality (territories, infrastructure, environments). Each link is a conditioning relation, not a direct cause; multiple factors at each stratum interact with the conditioning influence of power's exercise.

This framing prevents the misreading of cascade as direct causation across strata. Power does not "cause" deforestation; power's exercise conditions biological-stratum agents who, conditioned by that exercise, act in ways that condition physical-stratum outcomes including deforestation. The conditioning sequence is real and substantial; it is not direct causation, and the framework's analytical task is to keep this distinction clear.

## 6. The Subjected-Position Question

The v0.2 relations file concluded that power does not require a new field-position-type beyond allied/neutral/hostile/internal: "Unlike coercion (which forced `subjected`), power's four field positions mapped onto the existing allied/neutral/hostile/internal vocabulary without addition. Power is less target-directed than coercion."

The dynamics framework's analysis confirms this conclusion. Power's general operation is structural — it shapes the field rather than targeting specific agents. When power IS exercised in target-directed ways (a specific decree against a specific person, a specific exercise of capacity against a specific target), the exercise resembles coercion's structure: there is a target, a contingent cost, a recognisable subjected position. But this is the case where power is operating through coercion as its mode (REL-BND-POW-COE-001) — and in that case, the subjected position is part of the coercion analysis, not the power analysis.

The clean division: power's general analysis uses allied/neutral/hostile/internal. When power is exercised through coercion specifically, the subjected position from the coercion analysis applies, and that's where the analysis sits. No new field-position-type is needed at the power level.

This is a confident resolution of the deferred question. The conclusion was correctly reached in the statics analysis; the dynamics analysis confirms it without modification.

## 7. The Self-Justification Question Resolved

The deferred question, from the structural-type-analogy record REL-STA-POW-NUL-000:

> Power's conditional compositional character means it does not share the internally-consonant-externally-dissonant signature of coercion/captured-reciprocity/cancer. Power UNBOUND-AND-SELF-JUSTIFYING may approach that signature, but unbound self-justifying power is arguably a distinct value-flow configuration rather than power itself — a question for the dynamics half.

With the dynamics framework's binding primitive committed, the question can be resolved. Self-justifying power is **a specific binding configuration**, not a degradation of power's own structure.

The key analytical move is the distinction between:
- Power's own structural character (statics): conditional, decisive-without-being-directional, internally consonant when constituents hold together.
- The binding configuration in which power operates: variable, set by what power is bound to.

Self-justifying power is power whose other bindings (to reciprocity, legitimacy, authority, field-supportive patterns) have collapsed or been deliberately severed, leaving power bound only to its own perpetuation. This is recorded above as REL-BND-POW-SLF-001. Under this binding configuration:

- Power's own structure remains intact. The constituents (capacity over stakes, access, consequences, plus accumulation) still hold together; power's compositional coherence is preserved. The pattern is not "degraded" in the sense of having broken constituents.
- The field-effect is internally-consonant-externally-dissonant. Power amplifying only itself, without amplifying any field-supportive pattern, destabilises the field: counter-pattern formation accumulates, legitimacy erodes, reserves deplete, eventually leading to collapse. This is the externally-dissonant character.

## 9. Framework Refinement Findings

The most analytically valuable output of any stress-test is the surfacing of framework gaps. Power's stress-test surfaced several.

### 9.1 Binding-portfolio as configuration

Power has multiple simultaneous bindings (to reciprocity, legitimacy, authority, possibly violence, possibly coercion, possibly influence, possibly self-perpetuation). The framework's binding primitive handles each binding individually; the framework does not yet have a vocabulary for "binding portfolio" — how multiple simultaneous bindings interact and what aggregate character they produce.

This is a real gap. Power's character at any moment is determined not by any single binding but by the aggregate of all its bindings — a power-holder bound to reciprocity and legitimacy and (loosely) violence has different character than one bound to coercion and self-perpetuation, even if the same patterns are listed as bindings.

Proposed framework refinement: add **binding-portfolio** as a configuration in §4. A binding-portfolio is the set of simultaneous bindings a pattern has at a given time; its aggregate character is determined by the qualities, strengths, and durabilities of the constituent bindings. Portfolios can be high-quality (dominated by field-supportive bindings), low-quality (dominated by field-degrading bindings), mixed, or in transition. The framework should provide the configuration vocabulary; specific analyses use it.

This is a framework v1.2 candidate.

### 9.2 The cost-source schema correction is now overdue

The dynamics framework's binding primitive should be used to reclassify the existing cost-source relations in the coercion folder. REL-SUB-COE-PWR-001, REL-SUB-COE-GUI-001, REL-SUB-COE-SHA-001, REL-SUB-COE-OBL-001 are currently recorded as "substrate_dependence with character conditional" because no proper binding type existed. They should be reclassified as "binding with binding_kind: cost-source."

This is not strictly a framework refinement (the framework already supports it); it is a schema-update task that the framework's adoption now requires. Recording here so the schema update task includes it.

### 9.3 The temporal-scale specifics for power

Power's analysis used the framework's multi-timescale vocabulary (immediate, episodic, sustained, generational) without difficulty. The framework's vocabulary handles power's multi-scale operation cleanly. No refinement needed.

### 9.4 The binding-relation-side asymmetry

When the same coupling exists between two patterns and each has a relation record from its side (REL-SUB-COE-PWR-001 in coercion's folder, REL-BND-POW-COE-001 in power's folder), the records may not be redundant — they articulate the same coupling from different sides, with different relevant features. The framework should make explicit when such two-sided records are appropriate versus when one record is sufficient.

Proposed framework refinement: in the binding-relation type, add a `companion_relation` field that cross-references the companion record in the other pattern's folder when one exists. This makes the two-sided nature of the binding explicit while preserving the folder convention.

This is a framework v1.2 candidate, paired with the schema update.

### 9.5 Power's interaction with other power

The framework currently handles binding between patterns of different types (power-reciprocity, power-coercion, etc.). The case of multiple instances of the same pattern type co-existing in a field (multiple power-holders, multiple coercion configurations) is not explicitly addressed. Power-holders in coalition, in mutual constraint, in succession, in conflict — these are all dynamics-level phenomena that the framework's primitives can probably handle but that have not been articulated.

This is a gap worth flagging but probably not requiring framework refinement in v1.2. The framework's primitives (binding, accumulation, modulation-of-flow) should handle pattern-to-pattern interaction whether the patterns are different types or same type. Specific analyses (coalition dynamics, succession dynamics) will surface whether the framework is adequate.

### 9.6 Where the framework was confirmed

These confirmations matter as much as the gaps:

- **Binding primitive handles power's defining operation cleanly.** The single most important framework primitive does what was hoped of it.
- **Corrected amplification framing is analytically clean.** Power's amplification of bound patterns as field-recruitment-through-binding works without conceptual strain.
- **Accumulation primitive handles power's stock-formation across multiple kinds of reserves.** Both positive and negative accumulation are handled by the same primitive.
- **Cascade discipline holds for power's cross-stratum effects.** The mediated-transformative-delayed character of cascade is correct for power.
- **Agent-field co-constitution prevents drift toward agent-individualist analysis.** The framework's commitment held throughout the power analysis; the analysis did not slide toward "power is a property of the power-holder" framings.
- **DQ-boundary discipline held.** The analysis is static-pattern work about a dynamic phenomenon; no inflated metaphysical claims about what power "really is" beyond its operational character.

The framework has passed its first stress-test substantially. Refinements identified; foundations confirmed.
