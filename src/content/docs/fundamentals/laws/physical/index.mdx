## Scope

These laws describe the **physical level** of static value patterns: the stable structures by which matter and energy hold form, transform, and relate.

Physical laws are the most stable and least adaptable of the four levels. They change on cosmological timescales and form the material substrate on which all higher-level patterns depend.

In MOQ terms, physical patterns are not merely mechanical — they are the first and most fundamental expression of static value. They preserve what is, resist dissolution, and enable the conditions under which biological, social, and intellectual patterns can emerge.

## Dynamic Quality at the Physical Level

Dynamic Quality operates at the physical level in two complementary directions:

**Forming:** Dynamic Quality stabilizes into measurable physical order — pattern crystallizes from undifferentiated potential.

**Dissolving:** Stable physical patterns return to dynamic potential — entropy reclaims rigidity and restores openness to change.

These two directions are not opposites. They are the same underlying process observed at different phases. Physical laws describe the conditions under which forming and dissolving occur, not the force that drives them.

---

## Physical Laws and the MOQ Hierarchy

Physical patterns are the foundation on which all higher levels depend, but they have the lowest moral precedence. When biological survival requires the expenditure of physical matter, the biological pattern rightfully overrides the physical one. The hierarchy does not diminish the physical level — it locates it correctly.

| Level | Mode | Quality movement |
|---|---|---|
| Physical | Stability of form | Value stabilizing into pattern |
| Biological | Resistance to dissolution | Value adapting to persist |
| Social | Coordination of many | Value harmonizing across agents |
| Intellectual | Reflection and correction | Value becoming self-aware |
