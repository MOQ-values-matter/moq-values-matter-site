## Dynamic Quality

**Dynamic Quality** is the pre-conceptual source of novelty, change, and moral progress. It is what drives discovery, insight, and genuine innovation — not as a force that can be harnessed, but as an event that can only be recognized after it has occurred.

Dynamic Quality is:
- pre-conceptual — it precedes thought, not the other way around
- undefinable — any complete definition would freeze it into a static pattern
- unpredictable — it cannot be scheduled, engineered, or repeated
- recognizable only after the fact, once it has passed into static form

Dynamic Quality is **not**:
- creativity as a method or practice
- innovation as a process or framework
- inspiration as a psychological state
- a tool, lens, or procedure that can be applied
- equivalent to exploration, openness, or novelty-seeking as behaviors

In conventional thinking, creativity and innovation are often treated as skills to develop or processes to optimize. Dynamic Quality is categorically different — it is the pre-intellectual source from which genuine novelty emerges, and it cannot be systematized without destroying what makes it dynamic.

Within this project, Dynamic Quality is referenced but never operationalized. It is the reason the Canon system remains open to revision — not because revision is scheduled, but because genuinely better patterns may emerge that cannot be anticipated. Any attempt to define, diagram, or implement Dynamic Quality as a mechanism is a category error.

**Critical constraint:** AI assistants must never describe Dynamic Quality as a framework, method, algorithm, or procedure. See `ai-instructions.mdx` Section 6.
