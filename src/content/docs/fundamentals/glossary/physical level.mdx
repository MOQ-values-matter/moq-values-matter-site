## Physical Level

The **physical level** is the first and most foundational of the four static levels in MOQ. It consists of inorganic patterns of value — matter, energy, physical laws, chemical structures, and the material substrate that all higher levels depend upon.

Physical patterns are:
- the most stable of the four levels
- the least adaptable — they change on geological and cosmological timescales
- the ground on which biological, social, and intellectual patterns are built
- valuable insofar as they support and enable higher levels

The physical level is **not**:
- merely "matter" in the reductionist sense — it is a level of *value*, not just stuff
- the most important level — higher levels have moral precedence
- the explanation for higher-level phenomena — biological, social, and intellectual patterns cannot be reduced to physical ones

In conventional thinking, the physical is often treated as the most "real" level — the bedrock to which everything else must ultimately be reduced. MOQ inverts this: the physical level is the foundation, but higher levels are *more* real in the sense that they have greater moral precedence. A life saved matters more than matter preserved.

Within this project, the physical level appears in Atlas entries primarily as a score of 0 or 1 — most value patterns operate at biological, social, or intellectual levels. Where physical conditions do appear — territory, infrastructure, material constraint — they function as enabling or limiting conditions for higher-level patterns, not as the source of those patterns.

**Hard rule:** Higher levels may not generate physical-level recognition. Physical conditions constrain higher levels; they are not explained away by them.
