## Canon

**Canon** is the collection of stabilized intellectual-level static patterns that constitute the authoritative knowledge base of MOQ Values Matter. A Canon entry has earned stability through coherence, empirical grounding, cross-level integrity, and demonstrated explanatory superiority over available alternatives.

Canon:
- records the best intellectual patterns currently available under present conditions
- persists because it has earned stability, not because it is final
- remains open to revision when higher-quality patterns emerge
- is governed by explicit promotion, revision, and deprecation procedures

Canon is **not**:
- ultimate truth or moral approval
- consensus or institutional authority
- a container for Dynamic Quality
- a method or framework
- permanent or frozen

In conventional knowledge systems, authority typically derives from authorship, credentials, or consensus. Canon authority in this project derives exclusively from coherence, grounding, and comparative explanatory power. A Canon entry that a better pattern renders obsolete must be revised or deprecated — regardless of how long it has been Canon.

Within this project, Canon functions as the **intellectual static quality** of the knowledge base itself. The Canon governance system — including the promotion checklist, construction method, and governance document — exists to protect Canon from premature certainty, ideological capture, and intellectual drift, while keeping it genuinely open to improvement.

**Status progression:** Draft → Reviewed → Canon → Deprecated
